# Original-plan closure audit

Audit started 2026-09-22; updated 2026-09-23. This compares [PR01–12](2026-09-22-refactoring-plan.md) with the delivered source and acceptance evidence. It supplements, rather than rewrites, the historical [completion ledger](2026-09-22-refactoring-completion-plan.md). Current qualification belongs in the [follow-up results](2026-09-22-followup-results.md).

This is a dated audit. Physical CXL was restored after it was written; use the
[current release checkpoint](2026-09-24-release-checkpoint.md) and
[real-CXL report](2026-09-23-real-cxl-performance.md) for the present gates.

The owner selected local DRAM development, bounded retention with **no reuse or eviction of replayable data**, and Apache-2.0. Those are resolved scope decisions. Missing physical CXL is a hardware qualification gate. It is not an explanation for unfinished software tests or experiments.

Source 14 passed 53 Release CTest targets, seven linked TSan fixtures and 12 publisher ASan/UBSan tests; three earlier small TSan fixtures remain separately recorded. The manager-level allocator fixture has now passed. All 23 frozen live fault cases and all 52 fixed performance trials passed their runtime checks. Statistical qualification is narrower: one-broker primary ACK throughput remains inconclusive, so overall primary-throughput nonregression is not established. Source 14 also rejects a follower-only ORDER5 ACK configuration exposed by a retained failed workload run, and strengthens running-binary/source provenance. The [follow-up report](2026-09-22-followup-results.md) distinguishes sources, component tests and live results.

“Done” below means the software deliverable is implemented within the selected contract. It does not replace the integrated campaign gate. “Partially done” identifies an original deliverable or acceptance check that is narrower than originally written, not merely missing hardware.

## Acceptance that remains outside the finite throughput comparison

The fixed follow-up campaign tests one finite local ORDER5/ACK1/RF0 single-session workload with a common client. Its report uses the predeclared confidence intervals and retains the inconclusive one-broker primary endpoint. Even a passing nonregression signal does not close these original measurement requests:

- Small-message and partial-batch workloads; matched offered-load latency percentiles.
- Phase-level reservation/scanner/validation cost, saturation attribution and server-capacity isolation.
- High session-table occupancy and fence-churn performance, separate from functional capacity/fence tests.
- SMT sensitivity, same-socket and remote-client controls, and physical-CXL publication/cache-line behavior.

The first three are software/measurement follow-ups, not blocked solely by the absent CXL device. Remote controls require their own owned placement/provenance discipline. Fully instrumented multi-process testing is also broader than the successful linked sanitizer fixtures.

## Research and hardware boundaries

The paper's offset-only representation, recovery model and persistence contract must not be described as implemented by these refactors. The current prototype retains absolute shared pointers and requires a common virtual base. It does not supply physical writer fencing, active-head replacement, general prior-region restart, multi-host cache-coherence qualification, or full replicated-metadata/certified-prefix crash recovery. Payload disk completion and memory-copy token tests establish only their documented sink contracts. Physical CXL validation must precede CXL performance claims.

The completed software is materially broader than the earlier four-method extraction: it now includes the deeper linked broker/client boundaries, owned launcher dispatch, stronger live schedules, linked production publication/disk-failure fixtures, and successful TSan component execution. The exact remaining software acceptance differences are broader workload-equivalent historical migration in PR11 and the measurement matrix above, including resolving the one-broker primary uncertainty. They should be accepted explicitly as follow-ups or completed; neither the earlier tranche label nor a successful finite DRAM campaign silently closes them.
